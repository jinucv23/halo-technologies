Why choose an LED video wall

Simple, visible and built for daily use.

An LED video wall gives your business a large screen for promotions, announcements and video. Digital signage is the use of screens to share information and scheduled content; a scrolling LED board is useful for short text messages. We help choose the display format, size, brightness and control system for your location.

Match the viewing distance

Consider where people will stand and what they need to read. Pixel pitch, screen size and brightness should suit the viewing distance and surrounding light.

Plan content updates

Decide who will update promotions, announcements and videos, how often content changes, and whether one screen or multiple sites need managing.

Common questions

LED video wall FAQs.

Can you install an LED video wall outdoors?

Yes. We can recommend indoor or outdoor panels based on viewing distance, sunlight, weather and the type of content you want to display.

Can prices be updated remotely?

Yes. A suitable control system can let you update prices and promotions remotely. Multi-branch businesses can manage common or branch-specific content from one place.

Do you install scrolling LED boards?

Yes. We supply and install scrolling boards for shop names, offers, announcements and other short messages.

Do you provide service after installation?

Yes. Halo Technologies provides installation, configuration and local support from Kattappana.
