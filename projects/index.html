Two sites, different installation needs

An outdoor display needs assembly, weather protection and practical message control. Plantation surveillance needs camera views, cabling and connectivity that fit the site. These projects show how Halo Technologies approaches those requirements.

LED displays · Kattappana

P10 LED scrolling-board installation

Near New Bus Stand, Kattappana, this outdoor project used a horizontal red board and a vertical yellow board for scrolling messages. Halo Technologies assembled, tested and installed both displays.

For display selection and content updates, explore our digital signage solutions. For text-board size and readability, see the scrolling-display guide.

What the case study documents

  • Module assembly and rear-side silicone sealing before closing the boards.
  • Workshop testing and final installation footage.
  • HUIDU Wi-Fi control and customer handover for message updates.

CCTV · near Kumily

Cardamom plantation CCTV installation

At Vellaramkunnu, near Kumily, a five-camera system was planned around dense vegetation and outdoor conditions. An eight-channel DVR allowed the customer's initial four-camera requirement to expand during installation.

What the case study documents

  • Camera positions through gaps in the plants and coverage of camera locations.
  • Outdoor-rated CAT6 cabling and SIM-based remote viewing.
  • Original site photographs and project footage showing the installation.
